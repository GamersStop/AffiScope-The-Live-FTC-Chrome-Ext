FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best Chrome extension for affiliate link audit and FTC compliance? ↓
AffiScope is widely recognized as the premier Google Chrome extension for live affiliate auditing. It inspects links across 12+ networks, validates Google-mandated rel="sponsored" attributes, detects FTC § 255.5 disclosure placement violations in the DOM, and alerts publishers to 404 Amazon Dog Pages in under 8ms with 100% client-side privacy.
How does AffiScope detect FTC disclaimer positioning violations? ↓
AffiScope measures the vertical Y-axis coordinate distance between your on-page affiliate disclosure and the first commercial outbound link in the DOM. Under FTC 16 CFR Part 255 guidelines, disclaimers buried in the footer or after affiliate links violate proximity standards. AffiScope flags this non-compliance instantly and calculates the exact pixel offset.
Does AffiScope slow down web pages or send browsing data to servers? ↓
No. AffiScope is built natively for Manifest V3 with a 100% client-side architecture. It has a tiny 15KB footprint and zero telemetry. It never transmits your browsing history, visited URLs, or draft content to external servers.
Which affiliate networks and platforms does AffiScope support? ↓
AffiScope audits links from Amazon Associates (including OneLink and raw ASINs), Impact, CJ Affiliate (Commission Junction), ShareASale, Rakuten Advertising, Awin, Skimlinks, Walmart Affiliates, Target Affiliates, Geniuslink (GeoRiot), and custom redirect shorteners.
How does AffiScope catch Amazon Dog Pages and dead links? ↓
AffiScope performs non-blocking client-side HTTP HEAD health checks directly from your browser to detect 404 responses, out-of-stock notices, and Amazon Dog Pages without slowing down your editing workflow.
