Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about AffiScope architecture, compliance, and licensing.

AffiScope is built strictly on Manifest V3 and executes 100% locally in your browser. Its regex classification engine matches outbound URLs against over 12+ network signatures (such as Amazon tags, Impact irmp parameters, CJ IDs, ShareASale tracking tokens) directly in the local DOM. Zero web page data or browsing history is ever transmitted to external servers.
Under the updated FTC Endorsement Guides (§ 255.5), an affiliate disclosure must be "clear and conspicuous" and unavoidable. If a reader encounters an affiliate link before seeing the disclosure, the FTC considers the endorsement deceptive. AffiScope measures vertical DOM offsets to guarantee your disclaimer precedes any commercial link.
When an Amazon product ASIN is removed, blocked, or altered, Amazon displays a 404 error featuring photos of employee dogs (a "Dog Page"). AffiScope runs non-blocking background HEAD queries that inspect response headers and redirected ASIN landing states to alert you immediately of dead product links.
When AffiScope flags an unflagged affiliate link or one with outdated attributes, clicking "1-Click Repair" instantly copies sanitized, compliant HTML (rel="sponsored noopener noreferrer") with preserved anchor text to your clipboard, ready to paste straight into your CMS or block editor.
